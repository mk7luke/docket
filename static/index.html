How

4

Limits

  • A revisit is a day, not a clock time. The day follows the phone, not the server clock.
  • 80 decisions a docket. 12 new dockets an hour from one network.
  • An opened decision stays. You do not rewrite the outcome.
  • The owner link is the key. There is no account.
